\subsection{Strategic residual design and optimal harmonization depth}

Write
\[
\alpha=2-\eta,\quad \beta=1-\rho,\quad \delta=\rho-\eta,\quad
t=\delta g+\beta y,\quad b=\rho g+\beta y,\quad
L=\alpha+2t,\quad N=\tfrac92+3b.
\]
For a symmetric grand-agreement continuation, each country's Cournot-stage
welfare including its home firm's worldwide profits and once-paid design cost is
\[
 \mathcal W(g,y)=\frac{Nd^2}{L^2}
 -\frac{\kappa}{2}(1-y)^2
 -\frac{\lambda}{2}(y-z)^2.
\]
The private firm's unilateral Nash first-order condition is
\[
 \kappa(1-y^N)-\lambda(y^N-z)=
 \frac{6\beta\alpha d^2}{(\alpha-t)L^3}.
\]
When $\lambda=0$ the cost specification reduces to the original V2b model.

\begin{proposition}[Excess private residual similarity]
Suppose $d>0$, $2/5<\eta\le\rho<1$, $\lambda\ge0$,
and the full three-firm post-policy design Nash is unique, interior,
and symmetric at every grand-agreement depth $g\in[0,1]$.
If
\[
 \kappa+\lambda>
 \frac{180(1-\rho)^2d^2}{(2-\eta)^4},
\]
then the unique symmetric constrained social planner's design at a
\emph{fixed} $g$ satisfies $y^P(g)<y^N(g)$ for all $g$.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Differentiating $\mathcal W$ along a simultaneous common-design perturbation
and substituting the PRIVATE unilateral Nash first-order condition yields
\[
 \mathcal W_y(g,y^N)
 =\frac{\beta d^2}{L^3}
 \left(3L-18-12b+\frac{6\alpha}{\alpha-t}\right).
\]
Since $0\le t\le1-\eta$, $0\le b\le1$,
$L\le4-3\eta$, and $\alpha-t\ge1$, the bracket is at most
$6-15\eta<0$. Furthermore,
\[
 \mathcal W_{yy}=
 \frac{24\beta^2d^2(N-L)}{L^4}-(\kappa+\lambda)
 \le \frac{180\beta^2d^2}{\alpha^4}-(\kappa+\lambda)<0.
\]
The strictly concave symmetric planner's welfare therefore has its
unique maximizer strictly to the left of the interior Nash design.
The planner's symmetric restriction is essential: the claim is not an
unrestricted optimum over three asymmetric design indices.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[Adverse endogenous-design effect on sovereign depth]
Under the conditions of the preceding proposition, assume additionally
$\rho>\eta$ and a regular interior Nash branch satisfying
$K=\kappa+\lambda-\beta G(t)>0$, where
\[
 G(t)=\frac{6\beta\alpha d^2(5\alpha-8t)}
 {(\alpha-t)^2(\alpha+2t)^4}.
\]
Then
\[
 \frac{dy^N}{dg}=\frac{\delta G(t)}{K}>0,
 \qquad
 \frac{d}{dg}\mathcal W(g,y^N(g))
 =\mathcal W_g(g,y^N)+\mathcal W_y(g,y^N)y^N_g
 <\mathcal W_g(g,y^N).
\]
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Differentiate the firm's unilateral Nash condition implicitly;
$5\alpha-8t\ge2+3\eta>0$, so $G>0$.
The numerator $\delta G$ and denominator $K$ are strictly positive.
The previous proposition's exact welfare-derivative inequality gives
$\mathcal W_y(g,y^N)<0$, hence the second displayed strict inequality.
The frozen-design partial derivative is evaluated at the \emph{same}
Nash design. No ranking of different optimizing government depths is
asserted without separately imposing and solving the participation constraints.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Connection to government optimization.}
With the original bilateral-first, inherited-depth accession procedure,
the third-country participation margin is
$\Phi(g)=W_C^{IS}(g)-W_C^{SU}(g)$.
An interior constrained government optimum satisfies the appropriate
KKT conditions; when $\Phi$ uniquely crosses zero with negative slope
and $W_A^{IS}$ is increasing over the accepted depth interval, the
optimal proposal is the outsider's binding threshold under a
weak-acceptance tie convention.
The certified canonical illustration $\lambda=5$ in Draft PR \#18
has $g^*\in(0.05433320681,0.05433320682)$.
This is an exact algebraic root characterization, not a radical
closed-form formula for arbitrary primitives. The economic novelty
of the integrated mechanism remains to be cleared against prior literature.
