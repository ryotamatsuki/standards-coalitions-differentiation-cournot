## 2. Critical newly discovered predecessor and revised novelty boundary

**Lambertini–Rossini (1998), Props7–9:**
- The model distinguishes fixed-versus-adjustable quality/technology in trade liberalization and firms' Cournot/other competition. Quality/technology and government welfare coordination are evaluated jointly. In the medium-demand range **Prop8: government game produces no common technological/quality standard**; in the high-demand range **Prop9: government game common standard**.
- **DIRECTLY KNOWN** as an *economic phenomenon:* private product decisions plus international trade plus government standard coordination can yield both noncoordination and coordination; parameter thresholds can select common standards.
- **NOT shown by those propositions:** three independent government votes on an interface *depth* chosen before post-policy horizontal residual-design Nash; third country C independently chooses whether to join an already-formed inherited-depth AB union and has a distinct outsider continuation; **holding the entire government extensive form and private cost fixed, restricting only the firms' ex post design strategies changes the achievable governmental agreement**; or a complete all-g exclusion at theta0 versus a proposer-optimal frozen-IS option.
- **Interpretation:** V2b can no longer characterize its contribution as the first unified model where governments coordinate standards in response to endogenous product differentiation. It may characterize a sharply defined **controlled counterfactual and specific accession institution**, supported by explicit B/C design-incidence formulas. This sharply lowers any generic "integrated model alone = novelty" claim.

**Cabral–Salant (2009 WP/2014 final abstract):**
- Even more explicit than previously emphasized, WP Prop1 distinguishes *static* benefits from private dynamic incentives to standardize and Prop2 can find **incompatibility socially optimal**. Hence "endogenizing firm choices can overturn the static prediction of standardization" is broadly anticipated.
- However C-global in V2b is **not simply a new social optimum for incompatibility**: the *true optimized* grand standard is Pareto-superior to fragmentation at g=.05, but the AB→C path blocks it because government-specific intermediate fallback values differ. The *frozen-design restricted-game* offers feasible IS and the original model does not. This exact triple inequality / feasible-menu counterfactual is not in inspected Cabral WP (its consent actors are firms and its innovation horizon dynamic).
- **Do not claim** the mechanism is wholly different simply because private variable is horizontal design vs R&D: both are costly ex-post or intertemporal private adjustment that alters continuation value. Residual contribution is a country-specific 3-government coalition **implementation distinction**.

**Petropoulou (2008 WP/2013 abstract):**
- States and national standards are endogenous, firms adjust quality and prices, and international agreements cannot always achieve world optimum without transfers. So "policy chooses standards before private product feature and welfare-optimal agreement may be unreachable" is **already known**.
- V2b must emphasize the additional outsider's post-bilateral continuation **i(g) vs o(g)** and full-depth **m(g) vs s** plus same-rule design-restriction experiment; Petropoulou is two-country minimum-quality standards, not inherited-depth third accession.
