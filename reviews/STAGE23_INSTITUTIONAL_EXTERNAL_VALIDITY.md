# Stage23 Institutional Validity and External Scope (adversarial)
Date 2026-10-22. Evidence classification: official institutional descriptions, NOT estimates of causal mechanism.

## Required match
The original government sequence is an AB bilateral depth-g offer, B assent while anticipating subsequent C accession, then C and incumbents vote on enlargement at an inherited SAME g. The exact threshold uses rejection continuation SU for C, whereas direct grand proposal has SW outside option. This is an economically coherent extensive-form contrast, but its policy significance requires proof that a relevant class of real agreements restricts that degree of renegotiation or agenda change.

## Institution-by-institution checks
- ISO standards development: standardization is a staged national-body balloting / comment-and-revision procedure. A draft international standard can be approved with at least two-thirds participating-member support and not more than one-quarter negative votes. It is NOT universally a three-sovereign unanimity game with each outsider unilateral veto, and drafts can be revised. Source: https://www.iso.org/stages-and-resources-for-standards-development.html and current formal directives https://www.iso.org/sites/directives/current/consolidated/.
- ITU-T technical recommendations: sector members and states participate, drafts may undergo Last Call, comment resolution and further review. There is NO automatic mapping to AB-first inherited-depth and single third-country veto. Sources: https://www.itu.int/en/ITU-T/about/Pages/approval.aspx and https://www.itu.int/en/mediacentre/backgrounders/Pages/standardization.aspx.
- European common charging solution: the EU adopted legislative harmonization around USB-C; many portable categories since 28 Dec 2024 and laptops since 28 Apr 2026. Demonstrates that an interface may be standardized while other product attributes remain private, but is not evidence that a three-country accession veto caused or prevented agreement. Source: https://single-market-economy.ec.europa.eu/sectors/electrical-and-electronic-engineering-industries-eei/radio-equipment-directive-red/one-common-charging-solution-all_en.
- ChaoJi: EV-DC charging includes physical connector, communication protocol and system safety; the technology can be an illustration of harmonized and residual technical attributes. It is NOT an observed example establishing V2b's treaty-form government game. Official Chinese standards overview: https://std.samr.gov.cn/gfs/search/gfsDetailed?id=55FDD776577A6709E06397BE0A0A52DC.

## Most damaging referee inference
The original strict single-outsider veto and inability to reset g may generate the coordination failure by design. Direct grand voting reverses the rejection benchmark; much of Proposition B follows immediately from a previously established national welfare ordering. An example of successful standardization does not falsify a conditional game; conversely it does not validate the protocol. Current evidence: institutional scope CONDITIONALLY PLAUSIBLE as a stylized club accession game, but NOT yet shown to model the standard ISO/ITU procedures.

## Finite validation task
Identify a documented standards alliance / treaty-accession commitment in which existing bilateral parties set a binding interface specification/depth before third-party accession, later entry can be vetoed by affected parties or entrant, and reopening the initial design is costly or procedurally unavailable. Pinpoint whose outside option equals ongoing bilateral technical compatibility; distinguish unanimity from qualified majority. If the exact mechanism is unavailable, explicitly reframe as a narrowly defined theoretical club-accession game, and test whether an agenda restriction less extreme than perfect depth inheritance preserves a substantive wedge. DO NOT claim actual observational support if absent.
