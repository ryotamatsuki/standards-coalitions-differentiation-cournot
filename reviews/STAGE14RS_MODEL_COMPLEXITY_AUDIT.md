# One-extension candidate screening

Criteria: single independently meaningful primitive, ability to isolate the original V2b causal chain, nontrivial government-level prediction, shortest proof path, prior-theorem overlap. Screen below is directional, not an exhaustive search of all logically possible games.

| Category | Incremental attraction | Why not select |
|---|---|---|
| Private cost/type information / signaling | Imperfect Bayesian consent | Farrell–Saloner inertia and Farrell–Simcoe already cover information and standards-rule choice; Bayesian stage burden high |
| N-country generalization | Heterogeneous coalition sizes | Generic coalition-formation theorems likely absorb it; more variables not a new question |
| Asymmetric countries / firms | Realistic incidence | Parameter proliferation, no guaranteed new theorem |
| Repeated government bargaining | Credible punishment and cooperation | Folk-theorem and endogenous coalition background; new stationary equilibrium concept |
| Multiple investment periods / real options | Dynamic lock-in | Cabral–Salant and switching cost theory are very close; huge state-space burden |
| Endogenous protocol / multilateral proposal | Might remove sequential exclusion | Original grand-offer result already shows it; Aghion et al. (2007) directly studies protocol choice |
| Transferable utility / side payments | Compensate outsider | Standard Coasean surplus logic; original positive joint gain makes easy |
| Renegotiable agreement depth | Restore admission | Changes specific protocol and removes institutional contrast; general contracting prior art |
| Endogenous lobbying / political weights | Political economy reversal | Adds new welfare weights and agency structure; weak attribution |
| Firm veto / standards organization voting | Private implementability | Cheng–Huang 2025, Spulber 2019, SSO literature close |
| Patent licensing / royalty pools | Monetized strategic standards | WIPO and SEP economics already highly developed; extra market |
| Bertrand rather than Cournot | Market competition portability | Changes equilibrium architecture rather than resolving contribution |
| Consumer/product microfoundation | Interpret x rigorously | Improves foundations but unlikely new coalition prediction |
| Design bound, tax, subsidy | Government repairs private externality | Pigouvian logic, additional policy optimization, enforcement burden |
| **Residual-design adjustment curvature lambda** | **Endogenous firm rigidity makes government consent recover at a unique local threshold; exactly one scalar and no protocol changes** | **SELECT; priority challenge from Barrett–Yang and Cabral–Salant remains** |

Only lambda is adopted as a research proposal. Do not conflate gamma/eta/rho: eta network strength, rho technical weight, lambda residual redesign friction; x remains a single residual similarity choice. A single added scalar does not by itself guarantee genuine novelty.
