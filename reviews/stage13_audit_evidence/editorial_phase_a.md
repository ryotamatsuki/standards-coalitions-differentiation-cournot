# Editorial Phase A — manuscript-only
Read main text sections (Intro, model, quantity/design equilibrium, main results, welfare, nonlinear check, literature, discussion), not Stage verdicts or code. Prior exposure from prompt includes exact numerical claims, nearest-neighbor names, nonlinear failure and Lean boundary; not blind to them.
Independent central claim: letting firms redesign after a binding regional compatibility offer can switch governmental consent from feasible international coordination to fragmentation although a direct three-country offer would improve each country's welfare. IO interest: endogenous product differentiation changes incidence of a technical policy on domestic consumer surplus and home firms' worldwide rents, with an outsider's fallback converting incidence into an agenda-specific participation failure.
Contribution is a constructed existence counterexample / combination of established mechanisms, not a general sign theorem. Its all-depth exclusion is stronger than a one-point sign flip, but the economics relies on one exact vector and one inherited-depth agenda. The residual-design margin (higher x = more homogeneity, less costly differentiation) is economically legible, though why equilibrium design rises and why that hurts national exporting profits is asserted numerically more than derived intuitively. No obvious priority certificate; endogenous quality + compatibility veto is acknowledged.
First impression: honest restrictions and same-depth contrast are strengths. The paper's reading burden is driven more by repeated boundary/process statements than excessively technical mathematics. Main result arrives after model and continuation lemmas, appropriate; Intro not swamped by review. Repeated Stage/preregistered/certificate prose feels research log, especially nonlinear failed test discussion and final tool disclosure. Must not remove actual root/uniqueness bridges to shorten.
Provisional editorial stance: CONDITIONAL on contribution, not PASS. Need explain a nontrivial prediction relative to strongest endogenous-quality/veto predecessor, and clarify why inherited-depth bilateral sequencing is a useful class of institutions rather than an agenda selected to produce failure. Institutional counterexample is essential, not a robustness success. No FATAL economic defect demonstrated merely by existence scope.
