# Conditional publication strategy

At the present evidence level: **no journal submission**. Keep PR13's Level2 Route B alive. The adjustment-cost theorem is a bounded Route A opportunity, not a proven literature-first insight.

Challenge: Review of Industrial Organization, only after empirical/institutional interpretation, source priority closure and normal full-length mechanism exposition. Balanced: The B.E. Journal of Theoretical Economics, Research Article if mechanism and general theorem suffice, Note if scoped narrowly. Conservative: Economics Bulletin peer-reviewed Note or Preliminary Result if priority holds but full-paper explanatory depth would be artificial.

Recommended sequence if new theorem is independently novel: BEJTE Research Article first, RIO only if substantive impact improves materially; EB as defensible brief fallback. If adjustment-friction increment is absorbed, do not graft it into the original paper as a headline; revert to a narrowly advertised original consent contrast and assess its separate novelty.
