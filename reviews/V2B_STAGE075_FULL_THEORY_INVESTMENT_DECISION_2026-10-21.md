## C. Economic significance vs nearest parent claims

**Strongest honest editor-facing case (~160 words):**
Earlier standards/compatibility models already identify network gains versus rivalry, and earlier coalition studies establish nonmember vetoes. Cabral–Salant (2014) shows that standardization may deter endogenous innovation, while Takarada–Kawabata (2025) shows exogenous product differentiation changes regional mutual-recognition incentives. V2b isolates a different three-level institutional mechanism: two governments first commit to technical compatibility, a third has a participation option, and only after commitment do firms choose costly nonstandardized product properties that reshape Cournot substitution. Under a separately certified witness, international compatibility remains Pareto superior to fragmented standards for every country even *after* firms reoptimize, but the specified AB-first agenda cannot reach it because initial member and outsider incentives conflict. A government that freezes firms' product designs predicts the opposite equilibrium agreement. The scientific contribution would be to demonstrate why assessing interoperability cooperation needs downstream design response and entry order jointly, not merely to show that standardization may reduce private investment. This is a restricted counterexample, not a robust instrument prescription or a universal standards theory.

**Strongest skeptical field editor refusal (~140 words):**
The paper may add only a three-country coalition payoff table to the familiar idea that downstream incentives alter standards cooperation, with Rosen's standard concave-game machinery and a carefully selected coefficient witness. The C-entry derivative is twice the member marginal gain at the symmetric origin by elementary graph symmetry, not a separate new theorem. The government's offer sequence is highly specific; actual GSM/ChaoJi alliances do not follow the precise 2+1 unanimity rule. More importantly, the payoff reversal is so close to zero at the witness that nonlinear transformation of product design changes the constant-design C application sign at the original example depth. Nothing yet shows that the complete all-g SPNE regime inversion survives an independently plausible product-characteristic microfoundation. A perfectly checked interval program at one witness is excellent mathematics but not automatically a theory full-paper contribution. Absent more portable causal inequalities, this may be a useful research note rather than an IJIO/JIE-level paper.
