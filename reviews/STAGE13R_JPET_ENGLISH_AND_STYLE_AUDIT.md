## Editing acceptance criteria

A revised paragraph should state an economic claim, supply its reason or formal reference, and then delimit the relevant institution or parameter scope once. Do not replace every sentence, invent empirical settings, or use a different demand/coalition model to make the English sound stronger. Preserve correct theorem statements, the global certificate’s exact role and all nontrivial appendix bridges. Read the resulting article in one sitting after revisions to ensure the contribution remains visible without relying on the audit report.
