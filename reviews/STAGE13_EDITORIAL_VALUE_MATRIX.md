3. 非自明なのは、私的設計の反応による国別の利益帰属とCの中間的なSU外部国利得が、同じ政府同意手順の結果を逆転させる相互作用。

5. 深度継承・二国先行は結果に必要な制度仮定。直接三国提示で成立する反例はその必要性を明らかにする。これは制度メカニズムの識別を強めるが、一般性と現実での出現範囲は弱める。
6. 存在証明にも反例として掲載価値はあり得る。ただし従来の有力な結論の射程を変えるか、読者が現実に犯しそうな予測誤りを特定する必要がある。現在は前者の先行照合が未完、後者の制度的適用範囲も薄い。文章改訂で「よく生じる」「政策の実行方法」を付け足してはならない。

### 最強の推薦論拠（207 words）

The useful economic object in this paper is a government’s participation forecast, rather than the familiar private preference for incompatibility. A technical agreement determines one part of the product environment, but leaves firms free to reposition attributes outside the standard. Those subsequent decisions alter domestic consumer surplus and the worldwide earnings of nationally owned firms. The paper constructs a controlled comparison: the governmental bargaining agenda, country objectives, technology parameters, and cost accounting remain unchanged, while firms either retain their fragmented-market designs or reoptimize. International agreement is feasible in the former game and excluded at every positive compatibility depth in the latter. Importantly, that failure coexists with a conditional multilateral allocation that benefits each country relative to fragmentation. The outsider’s intermediate option explains why this is not a contradiction. The direct-grand-offer comparison also isolates the institutional content: the result warns against transferring a welfare ranking into a prediction of sequential consent. This is a potentially useful diagnostic for IO researchers studying interoperability commitments before product development. Its value does not depend on Cournot algebra being novel. It depends on establishing that the particular country-participation reversal is distinct from existing quality-and-veto results, and presenting the rival-design incidence channel clearly enough for researchers to recognize when the diagnostic might apply.

### 最強のdesk reject論拠（216 words）

The manuscript has not yet established that its counterexample teaches an economically important lesson beyond familiar endogenous-design and coalition-externality mechanisms. Existing research already shows that compatibility interacts with private product characteristics, that veto rights can sustain inefficient incompatibility, and that outsiders can obstruct broader coordination. Endogenizing costly horizontal differentiation can also reverse fixed-differentiation comparisons in published IO models. Here the surviving addition is a country-consent reversal under an inherited-depth bilateral-first agenda at one exact parameter vector. No economically characterized parameter region identifies when that reversal should occur; the nonlinear experiment changes the successful depth and leaves an extremely small frozen-accession margin. Moreover, the same governments would accept the welfare-improving agreement if offered directly against fragmentation. Thus the central failure depends on excluding that agenda, without showing why such exclusion is consequential in an identifiable standard-setting environment. GSM and charging cooperation motivate a sequence, but do not establish the relevant outside option or binding accession constraint. Finally, the closest endogenous-quality and veto paper has not been exhaustively matched at proposition level. Exact interval certification can validate the constructed example, but cannot supply missing economic priority, prevalence, or significance. I would decline external review in the present form, while leaving open a tightly framed diagnostic contribution if the closest-theory comparison and incidence explanation establish a substantive residual insight.
