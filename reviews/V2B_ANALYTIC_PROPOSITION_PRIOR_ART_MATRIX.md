## 1. Claims being tested and the absorption standard

| Claim | Exact object being compared |
|---|---|
| A | On a regular interior IS private-design branch, similarity rises with differentiation cost and falls with market size; its response to government compatibility has sign `rho−eta` under additional curvature conditions. |
| B | At the symmetric fragmented origin, a bilateral compatibility edge changes all three firms' designs, with members' similarity increasing more than the outsider's under the stated Jacobian conditions. |
| C-local | Fixed-design positive national participation effects can turn negative after private design adjustment, through domestic consumer surplus and rivals' effects on home exporter profit; local accession and bilateral margins are connected by symmetry. |
| C-global | At the frozen witness, private design adjustment reverses the government agreement outcome over the entire depth menu under the inherited-depth bilateral-first procedure, despite an inaccessible Pareto-improving grand agreement. This is an inherited computer-assisted theorem, not implied by the new local derivatives alone. |
| D | At `rho=eta`, the private output/design game is policy invariant, the country-payoff gains are linear in the number of technical links, and maximum-depth international agreement follows under admissibility and unique-continuation assumptions. |

A **direct absorption** verdict requires a theorem with matching hypotheses and a variable/strategy/payoff mapping; a common intuition is insufficient. Conversely, changing labels from quality to design or from firms to countries does not establish novelty. Incomplete source access yields **UNRESOLVED**, never a claim that a missing theorem does not exist. Mathematical methods (implicit differentiation, envelope reasoning, M-matrix arguments, Rosen uniqueness and edge symmetry) are not original contributions.

The displayed loss formula in PR #9 equation (9) has a sign error: the positive magnitude of the rival-profit loss must be **added** to the consumer-surplus loss. All C comparisons here concern the corrected formula, not the erroneous displayed expression.

## 6. What can responsibly be claimed

A should be a characterization/mechanism result, not a major claim that costs affect differentiation. B is the strongest new analytic candidate: a compatibility link changes firms on both sides of the agreement, with a conditional member–outsider response ranking. Its priority remains conditional on the unresolved source checks. C is the central integrated contribution: redesign changes the *agreement feasibility set* under a fixed government procedure; it combines known private incentives, welfare-incidence calculus and coalition comparisons into a particular equilibrium result that the inspected earlier propositions do not directly establish. D is a cancellation benchmark and interpretive boundary, not a general policy threshold or a third independent breakthrough.

The three contributions should be presented as one chain: **technical link → asymmetric private design → national consent margins → institutional outcome**. Neither “each component is known” nor “integration is new by itself” resolves the claim. The nontrivial additional evidence is the proven sign/ranking under stated conditions and the separate all-depth government counterexample.

Overall novelty verdict: **CONDITIONAL / SOURCE-SCOPED**. A source-complete assertion of standalone B novelty or C priority is **UNRESOLVED**. The inspected full predecessors do not justify a NO-GO absorption verdict; the access gaps do not justify FULL-PAPER GO on novelty alone.
