### What is valuable, and what is not established

The private FOC cannot remove resident consumer effects or rival-design effects on a home firm’s export earnings. The incidence calculation is short, economically interpretable, and tied to national consent. More importantly, the all-depth theorem rules out rescue by another depth under the same procedure. A Pareto-improving direct proposal supplies a disciplined institutional interpretation. This is enough for a reasonable editor to consider external review after revision; it is not a prediction of acceptance.

The paper does not prove unrestricted coalition instability, an empirical magnitude, a universal no-bilateral theorem, or a certified two-dimensional phase partition. The current text generally respects these limits. A few wording choices blur stand-alone bilateral welfare with the initial acceptance vote; the reduced payoffs also cannot be called unchanged across treatments. These are conceptual wording repairs, not demonstrated failures of the stated central theorem.
