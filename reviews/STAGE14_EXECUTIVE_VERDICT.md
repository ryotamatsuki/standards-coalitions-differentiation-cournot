## B. What is actually learned, ranked

1. **One candidate integrated economic contribution:** post-policy residual similarity choice can reverse national consent and eliminate every positive-depth union under the same bilateral-first/inherited-depth procedure that supports positive IS when firm designs are frozen. The strict center result extends to a **nonexplicit open neighborhood** of the original symmetric primitives by uniform derivative margins and compactness. This robustness corollary supplies no numerical radius or generally solved policy taxonomy.
2. **A clearer mechanism and targeted portability:** in the audited region firms become more similar, especially members. Consumer and rival-exporter incidence exceeds the positive direct member gain. A PSD-preserving nonadditive interaction between technical and residual similarity retains the **local** reversal on epsilon in [0,.001]. The amplification identity and threshold restatement explain the result; they are not independent discoveries.
3. **A sharp failure boundary:** actual grand agreement is Pareto-improving against SW and can be accepted if directly offered. The absence of agreement is SPNE under the stipulated agenda, not coalition-proof fragmentation or a general impossibility of international harmonization. This limits the proposed contribution while identifying the relevant institution margin.

## C. Decisive difference from predecessors

Government standards noncoordination with endogenous technology/design is already present in Lambertini–Rossini (1998). Barrett–Yang already studies private post-standard adaptation and public incompatibility; Cabral–Salant already shows endogenous private adjustment undoing static standardization gains. The candidate increment is therefore the **controlled firm-strategy restriction versus true redesign comparison of national participation constraints**, incorporating the bilateral outsider payoff. No inspected proposition directly supplies that entire comparison, but a narrowly unmatched experiment is not automatically important or globally novel. The prior-art report lists fresh versus inherited readings, exact propositions, WP/final mismatches, and unclosed sources.
