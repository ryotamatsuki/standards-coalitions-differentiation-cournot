# Referee 1 — Public economics, agreements and JPET editorial value

Audit date: 22 October 2026. This is a simulated independent referee assessment, not an actual journal decision. Manuscript read first: `stage25_jpet_v04.tex`, from the supplied v0.4 ZIP. Historical Stage-23/24 literature ledgers were inspected only afterward, and their favorable or unfavorable judgments are not adopted as evidence. This report addresses economic content and editorial fit, not independent certification of the interval programs or phase map.

## Summary

**Research question in one sentence:** Can privately optimal residual product redesign overturn national agreement participation, and consequently the government's optimal terminal regime, when its accession procedure and policy menu are held fixed?

The potentially publishable economic finding is not that firm choices create externalities, that product regulation changes design, or that a regional arrangement obstructs global cooperation. It is the controlled connection between three statements: national incidence of the residual-design Nash response overturns a positive fixed-design participation gain; edge symmetry transmits that change to the outsider's accession constraint; and the same change rules out every compensating positive-depth offer within the specified governmental game. The last statement, if independently certified, turns a local calculation into a policy-outcome result.

My economic-value answer is **YES, conditionally**: this is a defensible specialized public-economic-theory contribution, provided the all-depth result is valid for an explicitly defined extensive form and economically valid demand continuations. It is not yet an unconditional endorsement of JPET submission. No independent new general mechanism of international cooperation failure has been established. Its advance is a concrete, nontrivial incidence mechanism within a known institutional-cooperation problem. That can justify a paper; a new universal theorem or perpetual expansion of the model is not necessary.

### Why `D - L` alone is insufficient

The chain rule and failure of a national welfare envelope theorem are standard. The conditions `0 < D < L` inserted into a local proposition substantially encode the desired sign reversal, rather than delivering a surprising sign theorem without restrictions. The actual work with economic content is establishing those inequalities from admissible primitives and a genuine private Nash continuation; explaining why private cost savings worsen national incidence; and verifying that optimizing governments cannot escape by choosing another depth. A paper containing only the local lemma would look like a familiar policy-induced behavioral response in new notation. The open-region optimal-regime contrast is therefore essential to the present claim of full-paper value.

### Independent contribution explanation (128 English words)

International compatibility negotiations are usually evaluated against products whose characteristics are already given. This paper asks whether that benchmark can misidentify governments’ willingness to cooperate. In a three-country network-Cournot environment, private residual-design choices alter both domestic consumer surplus and resident firms’ worldwide profits. The resulting national incidence can reverse member and outsider consent under an unchanged accession protocol. The economically relevant claim is stronger than a negative derivative: a certified open primitive region yields integrated agreement with frozen design and fragmentation with endogenous design across the entire depth menu. A direct multilateral proposal remains mutually beneficial, identifying the interaction between private adjustment and inherited accession terms. The contribution is a specific implementation-failure mechanism and a controlled policy counterfactual, rather than a general theorem that regional standards impede cooperation.

## Major concerns

### R1-M1 — Describe the economic attribute rather than only its notation (MAJOR BUT FIXABLE)

In Section 2, `X_ij=(x_i+x_j)/2` means that raising `x_i` raises cross-product substitution in `B`, while `K_i=kappa(1-x_i)^2/2` makes a higher `x_i` cheaper. At the benchmark `rho>eta`, increasing a policy edge raises the effective cross term in `M`; this lowers output and weakens the private return to paying for differentiation. Private design responds toward a cheaper, more similar product configuration. Consumers and home-firm earnings do not share the same envelope property as the firm's own design choice. This is a plausible economic channel, but calling `x_i` only a 'higher level of the residual attribute' leaves the reader unable to assess it.

Minimum repair: explicitly connect the attribute to costly differentiation/similarity, state what observed residual characteristics the abstraction could represent, and distinguish this from a firm literally undoing the government interface standard. A documented institutional example may motivate the analogy; it cannot validate these coefficients or prove the result. Do not invent an application or claim empirical magnitude.

### R1-M2 — Institutional specificity is a substantive condition (MAJOR BUT FIXABLE)

The combination of bilateral-first agenda, inherited depth, and rejection continuation matters, not simply the word 'sequential'. The public-economic insight is that the outsider compares integration against its bilateral-outside position rather than fragmentation. The draft correctly states the direct-grand counterfactual and does not claim endogenous institution selection. That honesty preserves rather than destroys the contribution. However, a generic reference to bargaining delays does not establish the exact no-renegotiation/no-direct-offer procedure. Specify what type of agreement the protocol approximates and why accession and revision can be separated. Treat the protocol as a maintained restriction. No full new renegotiation model is required; the feasibility comparison already identifies its boundary.

At the policy subgame the vote order must be fully stated. 'Approval of C and incumbents' is not a complete extensive form if different voting implementations permit different equilibria. The proof referee must determine whether the asserted voting inequalities and optimal-regime result follow from the implemented game. This is a potential equilibrium-definition issue, not merely a policy-motivation issue.

### R1-M3 — The strongest claim must be readable as a paper theorem (MAJOR BUT FIXABLE unless the certificate mismatches)

Theorem 4 uses previous all-depth certificates, full-cube Nash regularity and an open-neighborhood argument as its economic headline. Appendix D principally instructs a future submission package to supply files, rather than presenting the actual certification proposition, interval domains, exact algorithm, and frozen evidence IDs. A specialist editor should not have to trust a sequence of research stages. The main text should state the few certified mathematical targets and show why they imply the policy result; the immutable supplement should reproduce each target. A missing exposition bridge can be repaired without new economics. A certificate for a different game/domain cannot be repaired by prose.

### R1-M4 — Priority comparison must center the controlled experiment (MAJOR BUT FIXABLE)

The full published Takarada et al. (2020) article already analyzes three-country standards and coalition blocking; its core result can favor regional over globally efficient standards under transboundary negative externalities. Kawabata and Takarada (2021), Propositions 4–5, show outsider losses from multilateral harmonization and a deep-FTA stumbling block. These precedents remove any general claim that standards can block multilateral cooperation. Their inspected government standards/compliance-cost/quantity games do not themselves supply the separate residual-design Nash switch under V2b's unchanged accession game. The draft acknowledges this distinction, but a compact model-to-mechanism comparison would be more useful than repeated assurances of modest novelty.

Neither a different extensive form nor an additional strategic variable automatically establishes economic novelty. Conversely, no payoff-preserving specialization or theorem mapping from those two papers to the controlled V2b reversal was found. That is a limited positive residue, not proof against every literature source. Final-paper/supplement gaps concerning other comparators remain with the prior-art audit.

2. Repeated self-referential phrases ('our answer cannot rest', 'an editor can reasonably ask', 'research ledger') make the draft read like an audit response. Keep the substantive qualification once, then present the model.
3. Retain Proposition B as the interpretation of A, not a second independent contribution. Move much of the strategically inactive `rho=1` boundary and its nearby strip to the appendix, retaining a short scope paragraph. Keep C1's local friction boundary if space permits because it connects the mechanism to a meaningful design restriction.
4. Magnitudes in Table 2 are useful for sign checks but uncalibrated. Neither the nonempty-open-set result nor the displayed 0.70 percent establishes empirical relevance.
5. Do not describe Figure 3's lack of observed SU as an absence theorem. The v0.4 wording appropriately avoids this; the numerical referee must verify the underlying classifications.

## Potentially fatal objections

No fatal lack of economic value has been established from the manuscript and the two closest published government-coalition papers. The following would materially change that judgment:

* A corrected government extensive form or economically admissible continuation eliminates the claimed all-depth controlled regime contrast. The residual local chain-rule example alone may be too thin for this paper's current public-economic-theory promise.
* A prior theorem can actually reproduce the same national-incidence and same-protocol frozen-versus-private-design reversal through a transparent specialization. Merely observing similar words or regional blockage is insufficient.
* The strong numerical/interval theorem is true only on a continuation formula incompatible with a well-defined consumer problem or genuine private best responses. Economic interpretation cannot redeem that defect.

These are conditional risks, not findings of failure by this referee. The mathematical and numerical reports must resolve them before integration of the final verdict.

## Recommended decision

**CONDITIONAL GO on economic contribution and public-economic scope; major revision before an overall Stage-11 GO.** This independent view is conditional on the other audits, not a vote that overrides mathematical defects.

### JPET fit and likely desk-rejection reasons

The current official JPET description covers theoretical public finance broadly, including externalities, regulation, voting, and public/social decision making. V2b's national welfare and international consent constraints fit that remit; tax instruments are not required. Takarada et al. (2020) is direct evidence of topical fit, not evidence that the present increment meets the editorial threshold.

The three most likely desk-rejection reasons are:

1. **Incremental materiality:** familiar private-adjustment and regional-outside-option ideas, with insufficient explanation of what the controlled all-depth reversal teaches beyond those elements.
2. **Economic/institutional credibility:** an opaque residual attribute and a narrowly specified inherited-depth agenda make the result look constructed to produce refusal, despite a direct agreement remaining feasible.
3. **Unreviewable main claim:** the all-depth/open-set theorem relies on external historical logs rather than a complete, stable computer-assisted proof supplement and explicit policy game.

There is a case for external review once these are repaired: the design-response comparison isolates a precise cause of erroneous national-consent predictions while holding the public decision game fixed; the direct-grand counterfactual explains why the resulting failure is institution-dependent. The paper need not solve unrestricted coalition formation or estimate real-world parameters to contribute on that narrower question.

**JPET versus Journal of Economics/RIO:** JPET remains defensible when national consent and institutional implementation lead the paper. Journal of Economics may better fit a paper whose sustained contribution is a specialized oligopoly/welfare comparative-static example. RIO is plausible if the residual design and network-industry interpretation become the convincing center. Changing outlet does not cure an incomplete equilibrium proof or invalid economic continuation, and this audit does not reopen a broad journal search.
