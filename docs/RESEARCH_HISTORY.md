# Research history and decisions (2026-10-20 to 2026-10-21)

This chronology records **what was proposed**, **why assumptions changed**, and **which earlier claims were retracted or downgraded**. It is not an endorsement of every intermediate theorem.

## Current conversation: sequential milestones

**A. Original task.** Countries A/B/C (one home firm each, all sell in all three markets). Governments bargain first over SU and a continuous standard integration depth; C chooses to request accession; A/B approve; firms choose product differentiation subject to a harmonization floor; Cournot vs Bertrand vs Salop to be selected. Standard compatibility raises consumer network utility; similarity intensifies competition.

**B. Choice of tractable primitives.** Proposed quadratic representative-consumer utility, linear fulfilled-network Cournot demands, `z_i*z_j` both in product substitution and intra-standard interconnection. Selected Cournot because linear FOCs and full homogeneity can be handled without a discontinuous Bertrand limit. No differentiation investment cost in baseline.

**C. Initial model permitted re-setting integration depth on IS accession.** Derived unconstrained IS welfare optimum:
- eta≤4/5: zero integration;
- 4/5<eta<6/7: interior integration `g=sqrt((5eta-4)/(2(1-eta)))`;
- eta≥6/7: full integration.
Suggested, but did not fully prove, IS path for eta>4/5. At eta=.82, g≈.527 IS unconstrained optimum; C could be induced by a weak *provisional* SU and then depth raised upon accession.

**D. User challenged the mechanism.** Critical correction: C may prefer remaining outside a highly integrated SU even though A/B accept IS, and an initial SU g≈.527 at eta=.82 is not attractive to A/B anyway. Because the temporary SU is pre-market and could be set to zero, an alleged 'A/B tactically reduce depth from .527 to .2' was not a meaningful equilibrium claim. Retain these corrections and never cite that tactical account as established.

**E. Crucial simplification: IS inherits SU depth.** User specified **NO RE-BARGAINING** on C accession. This is a substantive commitment constraint and changes equilibrium conditions; all downstream government conditions must compare `W_IS(s)` with `W_SU_outsider(s)` at the **same** `s`. The candidate economically-positive integration threshold moves from 4/5 to 6/7; candidate partial-depth IS region to about .884049, then g=1. These are model/selection-dependent and require final SPNE audit.

**F. Reality check.** User challenged the absence of positive-depth terminal SU despite existence of regional standards in the world. Observed that this is an implication of strong symmetry, unrestricted cross-border sales, zero adoption costs, and the special coupling of compatibility with product substitutability, not an inevitable property of Cournot markets.

**G. Rejected asymmetric cost fix.** A proposal to penalize C only with a stand-alone accession cost was questioned as ad hoc under ex ante symmetric countries. This was **withdrawn** as preferred baseline.

**H. Proposed symmetric extension.** All adopting governments pay the same real standards conversion/adoption function `K(g)=F+(kappa/2)*g^2` for g>0; zero when g=0. A/B pay when forming SU; C pays when joining, with no repeated A/B payment. This is ex ante symmetric but path-dependent in payment timing. A numerical terminal SU candidate exists at eta=.9, F=.163, kappa=0, g=1, if accession is vetoed and A/B find SU privately worthwhile. This shows logical possibility; not full robustness/general SPNE map.

**I. Empirical interpretation.** Model should cover *design-constraining compatibility standards* where common specifications raise compatibility **and** reduce product differentiation. Proposed examples: EPAL standard wooden pallets, ISO freight containers, ETCS/ERTMS railway signalling equipment. USB-C / ordinary interface-only standards are poor matches because interfaces may standardize while final products remain differentiated.

## Decisions
- Current primary game uses FIXED depth after C joins.
- Keep cost-free symmetric baseline as reference.
- Any adoption-cost extension MUST be applied symmetrically to all adopters.
- No extra assumptions solely to manufacture SU.
- Do not freeze, submit, or claim novelty until off-path global equilibrium and prior-art audits complete.

## Failure modes to preserve
- Misinterpreting an *unconstrained cooperative IS optimum* as C's *feasible join decision*.
- Conflating SW-equivalent zero-depth SU/IS with the institutionally unique SW label.
- Treating a symmetric trial profile as proof of all firms' optimal reactions without checking unilateral deviations.
- Ignoring Cournot corner outputs and possible multiple quantity equilibria for off-path `z`.
- Treating a derived threshold as a general theorem independent of the quadratic demand/network form.
