\subsection{Design first-order conditions and full asymmetric deviations}

Retain the notation of Section~\ref{sec:analytic}.
The three Cournot first-order conditions can be
written with off-diagonal matrix entries
$b_{AB},b_{AC},b_{BC}$ as
$Mq=d{\bf1}$. A unilateral firm-design deviation
differentiates only the two off-diagonal entries
incident on that firm's product:
$(M_{x_i})_{ij}=(M_{x_i})_{ji}=\beta/2$, $j\ne i$.
Therefore
\[
 q_{x_i}=-M^{-1}M_{x_i}q,\quad
 F_i=6q_i(-M^{-1}M_{x_i}q)_i+\kappa(1-x_i).
\]
For $\IS$, evaluating this unilateral derivative at
$x_i=y$ gives \eqref{eq:analytic-is-foc}; it would
be incorrect to differentiate the symmetric total
profit along $(y,y,y)$ instead.

At the symmetric fragmented profile $x=z{\bf1}$,
the off-diagonal Cournot coefficients are
$s_0=\beta z$. The spectral decomposition is
\[
 M^{-1}
 =\frac{1}{P}\left(I-\frac{s_0}{L}{\bf1}{\bf1}^\top\right),
 \qquad Q=d/L,\quad P=\alpha-s_0,\quad L=\alpha+2s_0.
\]
Taking the full asymmetric derivatives of the
three-firm pseudo-gradient and only then evaluating
at symmetry gives \eqref{eq:analytic-lh}.
The direct forcing of the AB interface link is
\eqref{eq:analytic-pr}, satisfying
\[
 p_0-r_0
 =\frac{6\delta\beta d^2(\alpha-3s_0)}{P^2L^3}.
\]
Its local reduced implicit-function system is
\eqref{eq:analytic-design-system}. Its determinant
is $(\ell-h_0)(\ell+2h_0)$, and the exact
solution is
\[
\begin{split}
 u'&=\frac{-\ell p_0+h_0 r_0}
               {(\ell-h_0)(\ell+2h_0)},\\
 v'&=\frac{2h_0p_0-(\ell+h_0)r_0}
               {(\ell-h_0)(\ell+2h_0)},\\
 u'-v'&=\frac{p_0-r_0}{h_0-\ell}.
\end{split}
\]
Under $\delta>0$, $\alpha>3s_0$, and
$-\ell>2h_0$, the eigenvalues
$\ell-h_0$ (twice) and $\ell+2h_0$
are strictly negative and $-DF$ is
a nonsingular M-matrix. Together with a
genuine interior Nash continuation, this
proves Proposition~\ref{prop:analytic-B}.

A conservative sufficient condition for Rosen
uniqueness in the whole asymmetric design cube
is available when $\delta\geq0$ and $\eta>0$:
every market matrix has off-diagonal entries
between $0$ and $1-\eta$, diagonal $\alpha=2-\eta$,
and inverse norm at most $1/\eta$.
Then
\[
 \big|\partial_{x_j}\partial_{x_i}(3q_k^2)\big|
 \leq \frac{18\beta^2d^2}{\eta^2\alpha^2}.
\]
If $\kappa>54\beta^2d^2/(\eta^2\alpha^2)$,
the symmetric part of the three-player design
pseudo-gradient Jacobian is strictly negative
diagonally dominant, so Rosen's strict diagonal
concavity yields global Nash uniqueness.
This does not guarantee that each Nash lies
in the interior; interiority must be established
separately for each use of implicit differentiation.
The new Stage13A exact rational interval proof
checks both sufficient conditions and the
local interior Nash continuation in an explicit
open primitive-parameter domain.

\subsection{Government welfare incidence and the corrected sign}

At the symmetric fragmented point the derivative
of domestic consumer surplus with respect to
\emph{any} firm's similarity is
\[
 \CS_{x_j}
 =-\frac{\beta d^2(\eta+2s_0)}{L^3},
\]
and the home firm has
\[
 \Pi_{A,x_A}=0,\qquad
 \Pi_{A,x_B}=\Pi_{A,x_C}
 =-\frac{3\beta d^2(\alpha-2s_0)}{PL^3}.
\]
Because $(x_A,x_B,x_C)=(u,u,v)$ under
the bilateral continuation, and because the
firm's own Nash FOC cancels only
$\Pi_{A,x_A}$, total differentiation yields
\[
 m'(0)=D+\CS_{x_A}u'
       +\CS_{x_B}u'+\CS_{x_C}v'
       +\Pi_{A,x_B}u'+\Pi_{A,x_C}v'
     =D-\mathcal L.
\]
This verifies that the loss contributions in
\eqref{eq:analytic-loss} must be \emph{added}.
The independently examined older Stage13 research
report printed an incorrect minus sign, which is
preserved in the audit ledger as a failed literal
formula; none of the present propositions uses it.

At the fragmentation-symmetric network, a country's
marginal welfare response to an incident compatibility
edge is equal across its two possible incident edges;
there is a common effect from the third,
nonincident edge. A two-member union adds only the
AB edge, while an international union adds all
three edges. Taking the difference of the
third-country's bilateral-outside payoff from
its international continuation cancels the
nonincident-edge response, leaving twice the
member effect:
\[
 (i-o)'(0)=2m'(0),\qquad
 (\widehat i-\widehat o)'(0)=2D.
\]
These identities do not claim a new generic
coalition-formation theorem.

For the frozen-design welfare, the
third-country's marginal bilateral outside payoff is
$\widehat o'(0)=D+6\delta d^2/(PL^2)$;
thus
\[
 (\widehat i-\widehat m)'(0)
 =2D+\frac{6\delta d^2}{PL^2},\qquad
 \widehat i'(0)=3D+\frac{6\delta d^2}{PL^2}.
\]
Under $\delta>0$ and $D>0$, C applies,
existing members approve, and B accepts
against SW, at sufficiently small positive $g$.
The derivative argument does \emph{not} prove
that proposer A's \emph{global} optimal
choice is this particular small depth:
that is a separate full-policy menu comparison
in Proposition~\ref{prop:frozen} at $\theta_0$.

\subsection{Independent exact rational nonvacuity certificate}

The independent Stage13A proof uses rational
coordinate bounds
\[
 z\in[0.7185,0.7187],\ \
 \eta\in[0.75795,0.75805],\ \
 \rho\in[0.89995,0.90005],\ \
 d\in[0.999,1.001]
\]
and defines $\kappa$ by the symmetric
fragmentation FOC:
\[
 \kappa=\frac{6\beta\alpha d^2}
 {PL^3(1-z)}.
\]
The proof treats every endpoint and interval
operation as an exact rational, verifies that
$\kappa_z>2.7166$, and thereby obtains
a nonempty \emph{open} region in original
$(d,\eta,\rho,\kappa)$ coordinates.
Among its conservative strict bounds are
$u'-v'>0.02709$, $D>0.00611$,
$\mathcal L-D>0.00737$ and
$-\ell-2h_0>0.76425$, together with the
full-space Rosen margin. The displayed
numbers are weaker decimal summaries;
exact endpoint rationals and the complete
no-float derivation are in
\texttt{verification/v2b\_stage13a\_independent\_audit.py}
and its logged \texttt{verification/stage13a/}
outputs. This proves the local design/consent
reversal on a genuine multidimensional
parameter region, not a global
$g\in(0,1]$ coalition-failure theorem there.

\subsection{The balanced-channel policy outcome}

If $\rho=\eta=r$, then
$M=I+(1-r)X$ is independent of the
government's compatibility regime and depth.
The strong curvature bound in
\eqref{eq:analytic-D-conditions} gives a
unique symmetric private-design Nash.
The second bound in that condition ensures
the symmetric design FOC is strictly
positive at zero, while it is strictly
negative at one. Its interior stationary
root is the unique Nash. At that root,
each country's consumer surplus increases
by $rgQ^2$ for each distinct compatibility
edge, without a private-profit or cost
response. With only AB linked, there is one
edge; with all countries linked, there are
three. The government's complete selection
result follows from the strict monotonicity
of \eqref{eq:analytic-D-welfare}.
