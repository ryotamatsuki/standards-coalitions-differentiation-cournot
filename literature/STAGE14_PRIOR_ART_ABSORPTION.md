Audit date: 2026-10-22 JST. Target: *International Standards Coalitions and Endogenous Product Design* (V2b), source `79b1a2a140754699a5a4c6d27aa469608e508bee` (PR #12). This report does not use the Private Compatibility research track. **Verdict: CONDITIONAL, not priority clearance.** The broad economic ingredients and several apparent headline results are already known; no explicit prior-theorem mapping proving absorption of the *precise controlled national-consent reversal* has been established. Unavailable texts cannot support an absence claim.

## 1. Exact claim and absorption criterion

The candidate is one integrated result: under the **same bilateral-first inherited-depth government game**, the original firms' costly residual-design Nash continuation makes every positive-depth bilateral proposal and subsequent accession unavailable at the certified witness, although a true endogenous grand agreement at a positive depth Pareto-improves on fragmentation. Restricting the firms' design strategies to their fragmentation choices, while retaining the same government game, demand primitives and once-paid cost accounting, permits an optimal positive-depth grand agreement. The local analytic B/C results explain the national-incidence mechanism behind that witness.

A direct absorption finding would require a prior theorem with a mapping of (i) strategy sets and timing, (ii) independent interface versus residual design, (iii) national welfare and outsider continuation, and (iv) the fixed-versus-reoptimized comparison and its consent signs. Merely changing player labels or adding a third country would not establish independent economic content. Conversely, a generic chain-rule or continuity theorem does not itself supply the primitive signs behind the V2b reversal.

**Claims excluded from novelty:** network benefits versus competition; compatibility distinct from other product attributes; costly endogenous differentiation; outsider resistance to enlargement; private adjustment overturning a static standardization recommendation; government standards noncoordination; domestic versus global welfare divergence; the implicit-function/envelope method.

**Hardest null:** once continuation payoffs are changed by endogenous firm actions, standard participation inequalities change. That statement is tautological and already implicit in strategic policy and coalition theory. V2b earns additional economic content only if its primitive assumptions explain **which firms redesign, whose national incidence dominates, and which implementable institution disappears**. The all-depth witness is stronger than an isolated numerical sign, but proof difficulty and the absence of an exact previous equation do not by themselves demonstrate economic importance.

**Assessment:** no direct absorption proof of the tightly scoped conjunction has been found; a global theoretical-novelty PASS is unwarranted. Significant claims remain vulnerable to (i) nearby final texts unavailable/deferred, and (ii) interpretation of the exact agenda-specific result as a small application of known private-adjustment/participation logic. These are separate risks. The second cannot be closed by obtaining more PDFs alone.
