## Research question → environment → strategies → extensive form → mechanism → theorem → counterfactual → significance
1. Question: inefficient international standards nonformation is not new. The candidate question is WHY nationally rational rejection emerges only when residual product attributes are chosen optimally after government depth.
2. Environment: integrable quadratic demand, separate gross substitutability B and fulfilled network demand M, three Cournot firms in three segmented countries, national CS plus HOME firm's world income, once-paid convex residual design costs. Each ingredient has close precedents; the particular combination needs meaningful predictions.
3. Players/endogeneity: governments first select H and depth g; firms next Nash-optimize distinct residual similarity x; firms lastly choose outputs. This separates g from x and from the inverse scale 1-x. `rho` is a weight, `eta` is a distinct network effect. Adding x itself is not novelty.
4. Game: inherited-depth A/B offer and later C/incumbent vote. Crucial rejection option under C accession is OUTSIDER SU, unlike direct IS proposal whose rejection option is SW. An artificially restricted action set could generate apparent failure. This is a live objection, not a hidden convenience.
5. Mechanism: let D>0 be fixed-design marginal member welfare and L>0 be the response incidence; national `mu_N=D-L<0` despite private FOCs. In three-country symmetry `(i-o)'=2mu_N<0`, yet `i'(0)=2mu_N+nu_N>0`. This is a controlled *national-incidence → dual-veto → agenda* chain, not merely 'network effects can cause noncooperation'.
6. Theorems: A is a controlled local and certified historical global SPNE regime reversal within a nonempty existential open primitive neighborhood; B is an agenda counterfactual, not a universal bargaining result; C1 is local lambda vote threshold, not global regime threshold; C2 has the rho=1 cutoff eta=6/7 with the rho<1 restricted endogenous-design strip.
7. Counterfactuals: keep identical government agenda and primitives while freezing vs reoptimizing x; then change only the direct grand proposal outside option while retaining endogenous x. This identifies two distinct sources of failure.
8. Significance: adds one possible implementation-failure mechanism. Limited by protocol specificity, network parametrization and small uncalibrated welfare differences. No field-wide priority claim.

## Direct formal absorption burden
No complete map of strategy sets, payoffs, equilibrium concept and theorem hypotheses has yet been established from any predecessor to `0<D<L` plus simultaneous outsider veto and an endogenous-vs-fixed **government SPNE type reversal**. Accordingly **DIRECT FORMAL ABSORPTION IS NOT PROVED**. That is NOT proof of novelty. Conversely, most components are already established. The only defensible claimed contribution is the conjunctive controlled national-incidence mechanism.

## Referee challenge
A skeptical referee can correctly say: 'Buccella already shows endogenous compatibility changes equilibrium; Cheng-Huang shows veto kills socially beneficial compatibility; Grossman et al. show design costs transform optimal standards regulation.' V2b must show why its **country incidence mechanism** creates an economically distinguishable prediction, not merely name governments as vetoers. Economic value should be judged at the mechanism and counterfactual level, not title words or missing identical notation.
