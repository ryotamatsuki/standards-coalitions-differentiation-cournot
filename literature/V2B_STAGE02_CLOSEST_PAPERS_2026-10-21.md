## Claim-level exclusions / downgrades

1. NOT NOVEL: network effects and standardization can counteract stronger within-standard competition (Economides–Flyer).
2. NOT NOVEL: firm endogenous quality/design helps induce or inhibit compatibility (Baake–Boom, Chen–Chen and related).
3. NOT NOVEL: outsider blocking regional-to-multilateral standards is possible in 3 symmetric-country models (Takarada et al., Takarada–Kawabata).
4. NOT NOVEL AS A STANDALONE: comparing network strength against product substitution (Toshimitsu 2016/2018).
5. NOT NOVEL AS A STANDALONE: national welfare and standards coordination responds to exogenous product differentiation (Takarada–Kawabata 2025 §5.4).
