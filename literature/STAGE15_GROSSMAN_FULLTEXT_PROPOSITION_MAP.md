**Particularly dangerous specific passage (printed p238, PDF page index 23, screenshot checked):** Governments' optimal standards under mutual recognition depend explicitly on whether private adaptation costs are **small or large**. When adaptation costs are small, firms meet destination standards; when large, exporters invoke mutual recognition and governments optimize for native firms. This is a published *design-adaptation cost → firms' choices → governments' standards-policy equilibrium* comparative-static mechanism. Therefore V2b may NOT present the qualitative thesis 'more costly redesign changes government standards choices' as new.
