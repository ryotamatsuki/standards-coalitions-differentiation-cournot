# Stage21 — Prior-art audit: can endogenous residual product design reverse standards cooperation into SW?
Date: 2026-10-22 JST. Status: **CONDITIONAL DISTINCTNESS; NO PRIORITY CLEARANCE**.

## Verdict and correct comparison
**Permissible precise thesis**: In the SAME three-country bilateral-first/inherited-depth government game with identical Cournot demand, national welfare accounting, primitive parameters and market access, restricting firm residual design to the fragmentation Nash yields positive international standards cooperation. Restoring the firms' endogenous costly residual-design Nash reverses the government participation constraints and forces a substantive fragmentation (SW) SPNE at the audited primitive configuration, even though a hypothetical grand agreement strictly Pareto-improves on SW. Verified Stage14 all-g certificates and exact local inequality.

**Not permissible**: "First network-externality model in which no standard emerges", "predecessor models always predict international standardization", "Gandal–Shy model turns from IS to SW after adding one x parameter", "SW is intrinsically efficient", or "the original government game disallows all Pareto gains even with a direct grand proposal".
