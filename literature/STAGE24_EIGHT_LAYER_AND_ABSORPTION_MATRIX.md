### 1. Takarada et al. (2020), JPET, P published HTML
Source https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/jpet.12425
1 **Question:** Whether 3-country standards agreements should be national, regional or global.
2 **Primitives:** three countries / 3 segmented markets / Cournot trade; mandatory standards controlling NEGATIVE consumption externalities, increasing firm compliance costs. Unlike V2b, policy standards govern *per-market* compliance, not cross-product compatibility link B=rho H+(1-rho)X and residual x chosen privately.
3 **Game:** governments determine national/regional/multilateral standards; CORE among countries rather than a given inherited-g SPNE offer sequence.
4 **Math:** equilibrium comparing all country welfare levels; nonmember can block MTA; no nested, separate residual design pseudo-gradient -J^{-1}f.
5 **Mechanism:** stringent harmonized global standard shifts outsiders away from preferred own standard, making global regime worse than regional status quo.
6 **Result:** full world-welfare optimum MTA may fail core; RTA stable when externality substantially transboundary; voluntary coalition block.
7 **Implication:** participation veto may obstruct internationally efficient standards, cooperation scope dependent.
8 **Residue:** V2b must be pitched as PRIVATE Nash design changes the *same* governmental participation vote relative to frozen design, and direct-grand-vs-bilateral agenda. The generic "outsider can block" not novel.

### 2. Kawabata & Takarada (2021), SOEJ, P published full HTML
Source https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1002/soej.12500
1 **Question:** Deep FTA vs customs union building/stumbling blocks for multilateral harmonized standards.
2 **Primitives:** THREE symmetrical countries & firms, segmented markets; Cournot, NEGATIVE consumption effects g(s) and policy standard s, tariff t, compliance cost c(s). W_i=CS_i+Pi_i+TR_i-loss_i. **V2b:** POSITIVE compatibility consumption network effects, no tariffs, government edge-depth g, firm residual x_i with convex design costs, W_i=CS_i+Pi_i.
3 **Game:** government sets common FTA/CU/MTA standard and tariffs; firms choose *quantity only*. CORE (not extensive form inherited depth).
4 **Math:** published Propositions 2,4. FTA always benefits outsider; MTA hurts outsider when gamma>0.91891. Under 0.91891<gamma<0.95757 only FTA is CORE (published §3.3).
5 **Mechanism:** tariff advantages accruing to outsider during FTA plus compliance cost of raising outsider's standard toward MTA. NOT V2b Nash residual-design adjustment reducing m'(0) below D.
6 **Result:** nonmember blocks global agreement; economic classification of regional coalition as stumbling block to harmonization.
7 **Implication:** multilateral negotiation and regional standards may clash; regime matters.
8 **Residue:** exact **fixed vs endogenous firm design conditional on same government game** absent in P; but B's generic implication is preempted, making A's incremental value the only plausible headline.
**Critical caveat:** The two models do not have payoff-preserving game isomorphism because firm strategy spaces and government choices differ; do not call A *formally absorbed*.

### 3. Grossman, McCalman & Staiger (2021), Econometrica, AV 2019 + 2021 publisher abstract
Sources https://www.princeton.edu/~grossman/New%20Trade%20AgreementsAug2019.pdf (complete 2019 author PDF 67pp) ; https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.3982/ECTA17536
1 **Question:** What regulatory cooperation is needed when national standards conflict with profit-maximizing product design?
2 **Primitives:** TWO countries; heterogeneous tastes for horizontal attributes, firms design destination-specific product versions a_i^H,a_i^F and incur convex fixed adaptation cost K(|a_i^H-a_i^F|); monopolistic competition and free entry; national welfare, tariffs/subsidies and, in extensions, local consumer externality. V2b has THREE countries and fixed global 3-firm Cournot rather than M-competition.
3 **Game:** products attributes independently profit-optimizing; governments can regulate import/local standards and choose agreement clauses. NOT frozen-versus-endogenous test under the same inherited-depth AB→C coalition protocol.
4 **Math:** AUTHOR-version Proposition1, Corollary1 (pp20–21): design choices socially efficient absent consumption externalities under corrected policies; Proposition2 (p23): selfish standards cause delocation; later externality case coordinates detailed standards.
5 **Mechanism:** home-country policy fails to internalize foreign impacts of regulator-induced firm choices; efficient agreements constrain opportunistic policies; adaptation costs important.
6 **Result:** need for regulatory harmonization depends on externality and institutional agreement; government+private design+domestic/global welfare divergences already researched.
7 **Implication:** claims "first firm product design endogeneity in transnational standards agreements" demonstrably unsupported.
8 **Residue:** bilateral accession-vote reversal via induced residual x and home export-profit/consumer-incidence is not contained in inspected 2019 theorem statements; 2021 FINAL proposition version check remains limited.

## Formal absorption test summary
- F0 **Demand isomorphism**: BFG 2023 two-firm fixed-design Cournot matrix is a boundary projection, PREVIOUSLY exactly checked. This alone neither proves nor rules out A.
- F1 **Strategic actor/set**: no inspected Takarada/Kawabata proposition introduces x_i∈[0,1] as *firm Nash choice*, with distinct govt g; missing necessary mapping.
- F2 **Payoff mapping**: Takarada/Kawabata W_i include negative externality, standards compliance and tariffs; V2b has positive network and convex design cost and no tariffs, so their numerical beta/gamma cannot serve as V2b eta/rho/kappa.
- F3 **Result mapping**: Kawabata–Takarada 2021 published Prop4 implies outsider rejection given regional outside option, a TRUE economic predecessor for B. It does NOT imply a *same-government-game* frozen-vs-endogenous sign reversal.
- F4 **Mechanism equivalence**: "regional agreement blocks multilateral despite joint gains" and "institutions alter standards outcomes" **absorbed at conceptual level**. "A known primitive-specific induced-design channel reverses both government participation margins" not directly published in inspected sources, but its local sign algebra is generic.
- F5 **Net economic novelty**: candidate narrower private residual Nash *national incidence and global optimal government-SPNE reversal* only, uncertain whether this constitutes a valuable advance beyond GMS2021 and Takarada2020/Kawabata2021.

## Eight-layer editorial take-away
Avoid two errors: (1) titles and similar outcome are NOT formal absorption of V2b; (2) V2b's extra optimization stage and parameters are NOT automatically economic contribution. The national incidence causal pathway is the only promising structural increment. Claim level: specific mathematically derived cause in an economically stylized two-tier regime and a nonempty original parameter-open neighborhood. Not: first failed standards union, first endogenous compatibility, or first strategic government standards with privately chosen product attributes.
