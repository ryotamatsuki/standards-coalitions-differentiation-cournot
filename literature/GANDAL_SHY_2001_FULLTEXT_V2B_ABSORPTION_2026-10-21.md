## Scope, game, and welfare (published pp. 367–371)

- Three countries, three differentiated brands, one brand manufactured in each country, and three separated national markets in which all firms compete (published p. 367).
- Buyers have **inelastic unit demand** on a Salop circle with **fixed, equally spaced firms**, quadratic travel/disutility term. Government recognition can affect which standards interoperate; but **firms do not choose their variety location, horizontal differentiation, quality or design** (p. 368, Eq. 1 and §2.2).
- **First** governments choose whether to recognize foreign standards; **then** firms compete on **prices (Bertrand)** separately in national markets and buyers purchase (p. 369 §2.3; p. 381 Appendix A). There is **no intermediate post-government endogenous firm differentiation/design investment**.
- If a foreign standard is not recognized, foreign firms pay a conversion cost to access the local market (pp. 368–369). Alternatively, recognition raises gross consumption value through compatibility with *other branded products*; compatible pairs confer additive utility increment `delta` (published p. 368), **not an endogenous quantities-based fulfilled-network-expectations fixed point**.
- Country welfare is domestic consumer surplus plus worldwide profits of its home firm (p. 371, Eq. 11): substantially close to V2b in the *government objective*, but with no V2b once-paid product-design resource cost.
- They solve **two polar cases**, (i) conversion costs without network gains (§3), (ii) network gains without conversion costs (§4), then discuss the joint possibility qualitatively (§5). They do not solve one model where network gains and competitive substitution are separately regulated by a continuous compatibility g and firms select an additional x.

## Published propositions and sign restrictions

| Published result | Exact domain/claim (verified against original) | Relevance to V2b |
|---|---|---|
| **Proposition 1, p. 372** | If each government may either recognize **all** foreign standards R or **none** NR, and conversion costs satisfy `c<5`, recognizing all is a dominant governmental strategy (cost-only case). | Already covers the unilateral-government *recognition* baseline. It does not refer to post-policy firm design. |
| **Proposition 2, p. 375** | In cost-only case: `c<5/4` full mutual recognition makes every country better off than a two-country union; `5/4<=c<=5/2` a two-country SU gives members more than the no-union benchmark; excluded country prefers recognizing all other standards. | **Government SU and outsider incentive pre-existing**; do not claim novelty for three-country alliance structure, excluded-country response or network-union comparisons. |
| **Proposition 3, p. 375** | `5/2<c<5`: SU raises a member's welfare relative to mutual recognition, by foreclosing the nonmember from member markets. | Positive SU from trade diversion/cost-raising predates V2b. Different cost and competitive mechanism. |
| **Proposition 4, p. 378** | Network-only case (zero conversion costs): R is a dominant strategy over the binary R/NR set. Under full R versus NR, prices, market shares and profits are unaffected; gross consumer network value changes. | Positive compatibility and full recognition naturally favored **in their network-only fixed-differentiation Bertrand model**. Not an invariant under V2b's policy-contingent cross-substitution & design response. |
| **Proposition 5, p. 379** | For moderate network value `delta<5/2`, **universal mutual recognition** gives each country higher welfare than a two-country SU. §4.2.2 extends full-recognition > two-country SU member ranking to `5/2<delta<5`. | Their `no exclusionary SU` **does not imply no interconnection**: all three standards are mutually recognized even when there is no formal SU. No same-hypotheses contradiction with V2b's positive-depth non-integration result. |
| **Conclusion, pp. 379–380** | Conversion-cost-driven exclusionary unions may divert trade; network effects discourage such discriminatory unions; joint case depends on relative forces. | Broad standards-network versus strategic fragmentation motivation **known**. |

**Key important false-equivalence:** In Gandal–Shy, **“no standardization union” under benchmark government R choices yields worldwide *mutual recognition and compatible standards***. In V2b, **SW means no bilateral technical compatibility** (H off-diagonals all zero), with g=0 substantive no integration. Thus `GS no SU ≠ V2b SW`; and `GS all R ≠ V2b no-standardization`. Never read network-only Proposition 5 as a proof of V2b's zero-compatibility outcome, nor claim V2b refutes Gandal–Shy.

## Direct theorem-absorption map

1. **Government action mapping.** GS R/NR and discriminatory SU on a directed international recognition pattern ↔ V2b governments choose AB graph link at continuous depth g and C can seek IS accession at the SAME inherited g. **PARTIAL ONLY:** GS lacks a continuous g and this specific A-offer/B-consent/C-accession/unanimous vote sequence.
2. **Firm strategy mapping.** GS Bertrand price after fixed exogenous Salop differentiation ↔ V2b an intervening Nash game in `x_i∈[0,1]` with cost `κ(1-x_i)²/2`, followed by Cournot quantity. **NO EXACT MAPPING:** GS has no firm strategy or parameter counterpart to x or κ; setting x as a fixed parameter does not produce G&S demand (unit demand circle and Bertrand).
3. **Network mapping.** GS compatible-brand *additive utility shift* δ ↔ V2b ηH multiplied by expected/equilibrium quantities; V2b B also has direct `ρH` substitutability. **NO EXACT MAPPING** preserving quantity or price equilibrium and government welfare.
4. **Welfare mapping.** Both governments maximize domestic CS plus their own firms' aggregate world profit, a **SUBSTANTIVE SAME OBJECTIVE FAMILY**. V2b additionally deducts real product-design expenses and firms' policy-responsive substitution. General government welfare account is not novel.
5. **Claim/output mapping.** GS Props 1–5 give recognition dominance, cost-threshold exclusionary SU and network-only superiority of full mutual recognition over discriminatory SU. They do **not** state or directly imply the **controlled** V2b comparison `frozen x→positive-depth IS`, `endogenous x→no positive-depth agreement` or simultaneous flip of A/B SU and C IS joining margins.
