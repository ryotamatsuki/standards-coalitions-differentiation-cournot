## Journal-specific manuscript architecture, minimum reader-cost
**Positioning sentence**: Endogenous, noncooperative residual private product redesign following negotiated compatibility can reverse national consent incentives **relative to an identical governmental game with frozen private design**, even though directly negotiated multilateral standards offer a Pareto-improving outcome under the specified primitive region; the equilibrium obstruction requires inherited-depth sequential accession. Do not label 'first standards bloc veto' or 'first gov standards and private design'.
**Title provisional**: `Endogenous Product Design and Participation in International Compatibility Agreements`; current working title `International Standards Coalitions and Endogenous Product Design` also usable. Avoid over-claiming "impossibility of multilateral standardization".
**Article type** ORIGINAL ARTICLE, not comments on Takarada 2020, not note unless manuscript actually shortens, not policy case study.
**Sections**: 1 Introduction (government commitment and firm Nash x; falsifiable incremental argument vs Takarada 2020, Kawabata-Takarada 2021, Grossman-McCalman-Staiger 2021); 2 Institutional environment & model (A chooses g, sequential AB→C, inherited g, no de novo grand or renegotiation in BASELINE; firm x Nash AFTER govt choices, firms sell to three segmented markets, exact W_i); 3 Multi-stage equilibria; 4 Central Proposition A (local D-L and globally certified nonempty primitive-open set, fixed-design controlled benchmark); 5 Institutional comparison (direct-grand, limitations, Proposition B not general new theorem); 6 Boundaries/robustness (lambda local critical vs global example, rho→1 eta=6/7 face not strategic at face); 7 Conclusion; math Appendix & verifiable CI.
**Notation/exposition**: use g vs x_i vs 1-x_i, rho compatibility mix, consumer surplus plus WORLDwide home firm profit; do not conflate frozen Nash at SW with optimum at g>0; do not claim real ISO rounds locked; cite real sequential-accession cases as motivation ONLY, not identification/calibration. No endogenously chosen negotiation cost unless new model certified. No coalition-proof assertion.
**Core visual/exhibit plan**: one rigorous marginal-incidence D vs L schematic or small numerical-table g=.05 signs; one agenda-vote diagram IF reduces reader effort; proposition table for local/global and restrictions; reproducibility appendix. No gratuitous charts or made-up calibrated magnitudes.
