## Editorial contribution statement (maximum licensed)
A government's assessment of an international compatibility agreement can have the **wrong sign** if it treats firms' costly residual product differentiation as fixed. Under a specified AB-first, inherited-depth three-country accession protocol, the fixed-design and noncooperative private-Nash continuations are evaluated with the *same* governmental strategies and welfare definition. At a certified parameter center and within an unquantified nonempty open neighborhood, the latter shifts the government-optimal terminal agreement from positive integrated standards (IS) to no substantive accord (SW); the direct-grand counterfactual can nevertheless benefit all countries. The mechanism is incidence of the private design response on domestic consumer surplus and home firm worldwide profits. This does NOT invent regional blockage, government regulation/endogenous firm design, compatible externalities, or veto games; those broad ideas exist in prior art. The local chain rule D−L and symmetry edge lemma alone are insufficient editorial contributions. The **all-g same-protocol policy-regime contrast** is the original model-specific analytical payoff.

**Stage7.5A scope fixed:** INSTITUTION-SPECIFIC on the government game, CONDITIONALLY PORTABLE as existential result across **two separately certified** residual design functions `phi=x,x²`. ## Serious JPET editorial test — not positivity theater
### Most likely desk-rejection text (hypothetical)
“While mathematically careful, the paper combines known private design response in regulation with well-established regional standards blocking. The sign reversal rests on three symmetric Cournot firms and an inherited-depth offer structure with neither negotiated terms nor endogenous bargaining frictions. Why is this example a substantive new insight in public economics rather than a variant of earlier standards-coalition models?”

### Our best *licensed*, noninvented answer
“Unlike earlier regional-versus-multilateral standards comparisons, the analysis **holds the entire government game fixed** and compares the national consent and government-optimal terminal outcomes when downstream residual design is held at fragmentation Nash versus when firms reoptimize noncooperatively. National welfare values both one country's consumers and the home firm's profit in all markets; private own-profit optimality does not neutralize other design incidence. Verified derivative inequalities eliminate every compensation-by-depth offer at the certified center, while the direct-grand counterfactual isolates institution-specific impediment. This predicts when frozen-technology national policy appraisal can qualitatively misclassify implementable multilateral cooperation.”

This is a defensible editorial *reason to send for review*, not a guarantee of sufficient external-referee novelty. The scope is narrow/moderate significance, methodologically exact, institutionally conditional. Existing sources do NOT show a direct theorem-level absorption of this entire controlled conjunction; some inaccessible BFG/CH/Klimenko supplemental propositions remain unclosed and cannot be claimed checked. If a concrete theorem absorber later surfaces, reopen relevant Stage6/11, not misrepresent Stage12.
